## 1.1.0

- **Better Combat.** Added optional melee combos with dedicated first- and third-person animations for Glaive Prime, Redeemer Prime, and Quassus Prime. Redeemer uses a left slash, right slash, and thrust.
- **Yagens-Attributes.** Added optional weapon profiles with critical stats, status chances, damage distributions, and Riven dispositions. Special attacks use their own profiles and retain the weapon's Mod bonuses from launch. Pellets and blades roll critical hits and status effects individually. Glaive throws and detonations force Slash and Impact procs, and Quassus blades force Slash procs.
- **Glaive Prime.** Detonation uses custom area damage that bypasses vanilla explosion immunity.
- **Redeemer Prime.** Extended the shotgun special attack's forward range from 32 to 64 blocks.
