## 1.0.0

- **Core rewrite.** Rebuilt Tennory for NeoForge 1.21.1, moving weapon stats and spread rules into a shared module for future ports.
- **Glaive Prime.** Reworked charged throws, ricochets off blocks and mobs, and mid-flight detonation. Added first-impact detonation in Volatile mode and server-side recovery if the thrown glaive goes missing.
- **Redeemer Prime.** Rebuilt the gunblade special attack as a ten-pellet shotgun blast with three independently recharging chambers.
- **Quassus Prime.** Added dual warfans that launch horizontal volleys of 18 and 9 blades, with independent recharge timers. Pellets and blades disappear on first contact with a block or mob.
- **Models and animations.** Updated the weapon models and added dedicated first- and third-person attack animations and projectile effects.
- **Loot.** Added an equal-weight weapon pool with independent bonus rolls at Ominous Vaults (7.5% per opening) and Ominous Trial Spawners (2% per consumable reward).
